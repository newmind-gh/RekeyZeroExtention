# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RekeyZero contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Participation is welcome regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint when expressed constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and observable behavior.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes.
- Protect private information and never publish credentials or customer data.
- Respect project safety, security, and disclosure processes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or sustained personal attacks.
- Doxxing or publishing private communications without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith manipulation.
- Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, release channels, and other spaces where a person represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **rekeyzero@outlook.com**. Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

The maintainer will review reports confidentially where possible, consider context and impact, and may issue a warning, remove content, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Conflicts involving the maintainer should be documented and escalated to an independent repository administrator when one is available.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.
